## Integrity and Authentication

This module introduces the core ideas behind cryptographic integrity: hash functions, their security properties, and message authentication codes. The early challenges focus on computing SHA-3 family digests and understanding preimage, collision, and length-extension behavior.

The later challenges move into CBC-MAC and HMAC. The emphasis there is on recognizing when a construction is sound, when it is not, and how seemingly small design mistakes can make forgery practical.
